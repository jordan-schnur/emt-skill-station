# Station Guide Sources

Station guides are practice references for NREMT exam stations that do not have an official NREMT skill sheet PDF. Steps reflect current EMT-Basic scope of practice.

## References

- **AAOS Emergency Care and Transportation of the Sick and Injured, 12th Edition** — Primary step sequencing reference for CPAP, Vitals, and Suctioning
- **Brady Emergency Medical Responder: Your First Response in Emergency Care, 7th Edition** — Secondary reference for vital sign technique and airway management
- **NREMT EMT Psychomotor Examination Content Outline** (nremt.org) — Scope and station format reference
- **American Heart Association BLS Provider Manual, 2020** — CPR/airway integration standards
- **ACEP Clinical Policy: Critical Issues in the Evaluation and Management of Adult Patients Presenting to the Emergency Department with Acute COPD** — CPAP indication criteria
- **NAEMSP Position Statement on 12-Lead ECG in EMS** — 12-lead acquisition standards and lead placement
